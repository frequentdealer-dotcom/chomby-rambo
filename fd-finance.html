Accounting Principles

Capital is not sales revenue. Drawings are not operating expenses. Allocated funds are not expenses until spent. Unverified figures must remain flagged.

Management Routine

Daily: record transactions.
Weekly: review budgets and inventory.
Monthly: prepare reports and assess performance.
